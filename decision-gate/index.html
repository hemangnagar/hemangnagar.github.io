Challenge the decision. Then make one.

Decision Gate turns an AI argument into a bounded decision process: identify what must be true, attack the load-bearing assumptions, stop when further review has low value, and force an ACT / WAIT / ABANDON call.

The models generate reasoning. They do not control the final gate.

The control boundary is the point.

USER DECISION ↓ BUILDER MODEL extracts claims / dependencies ↓ ADVERSARY MODEL challenges only consequential assumptions ↓ BOUNDED REVIEW max rounds OR no new material challenge ↓ DETERMINISTIC GATE unresolved FATAL → ABANDON unresolved BLOCKING → WAIT otherwise → ACT + accepted risks ↓ COMMIT LEDGER timestamp + stopping reason + resolution recipes ↓ REOPEN / OUTCOME only declared triggers reopen; reality scores reasoning

What is implemented

Cross-model adversarial runner

Builder and adversary roles are provider-neutral through LiteLLM. The model strings are user-selected rather than hard-coded.

Deterministic sufficiency gate

Final ACT / WAIT / ABANDON is computed from unresolved challenge materiality, not generated by another model.

Controlled reopening

A closed review does not restart because another model found another objection. Reopening requires a declared trigger tied to the ledger.

Outcome scoring

Later observations are recorded against original claims and accepted risks so decision quality can eventually be judged by reality.

Why it exists

Adversarial agents can argue forever. Decision Gate treats termination as a first-class part of reasoning: challenge enough to act intelligently, commit, and then stop talking until something material changes.
